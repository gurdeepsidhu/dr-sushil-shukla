Profile

Dr. Sushil Shukla

Dr. Sushil Shukla is a highly accomplished Pediatric Cardiologist and Interventional Cardiology specialist with over 20 years of extensive experience in clinical pediatrics and congenital heart disease management.

For the past 15+ years, Dr. Shukla has dedicated his career specifically to pediatric cardiology and specialized catheter-based interventions. He currently serves as a Sr. Consultant of Pediatric Interventional Cardiology, consulting across leading heart centers in Delhi NCR, India.

Dr. Shukla has accumulated extensive clinical experience working in premier healthcare facilities in both India and Ireland. His expertise covers advanced diagnostic cardiac catheterizations, complex neonatal interventions, and congenital stenting.
