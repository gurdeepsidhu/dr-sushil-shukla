Specialization

Conditions Managed

Congenital Heart Disease (CHD)

Comprehensive care for structural heart issues, from simple atrial septal defects (ASD) to complex single-ventricle anomalies, coordinating with cardiac surgeons for hybrid procedures.

View Interventional Treatments

Neonatal Cardiac Interventions

Critical, life-saving catheter interventions in newborns including balloon atrial septostomy and ductal stenting for severe congenital heart lesions.

Learn about ICU and Surgery support

Fetal Cardiology

Advanced diagnostic fetal echocardiograms starting at 18 weeks of gestation, with high-accuracy prenatal detection and parental counseling.

Pulmonary Hypertension

Diagnostic evaluation and customized medical therapies for children presenting with elevated pulmonary pressures associated with congenital defects.

Adult Congenital Heart Disease (ACHD)

Long-term medical monitoring and specialized catheter interventions for adult survivors who were born with heart anomalies.

Complex Structural Interventions

Advanced transcatheter device occlusion (ASD, VSD, PDA), hybrid interventions, and stent implantations for multi-lesion congenital anomalies.
