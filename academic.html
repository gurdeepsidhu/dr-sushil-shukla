Education & Training

Academic Teaching

Dr. Sushil Shukla is highly committed to educating and training the next generation of pediatricians and pediatric cardiologists.

His academic contributions include training pediatric fellows in congenital echocardiography, teaching catheterization methodologies, and lecturing at regional workshops and pediatric care panels in India and Ireland.

Clinical Investigation

Research & Interests

Dr. Shukla's clinical research interests focus on improving procedural safety and outcomes in transcatheter device closures for congenital anomalies, optimizing neonatal emergency catheterizations, and advances in fetal echocardiography screening.

Areas of Research Interest
  • Minimally invasive device closures (ASD, VSD, PDA)
  • Ductal stenting and emergency valvuloplasties in neonates
  • Long-term follow-up studies in adult congenital survivors (ACHD)
