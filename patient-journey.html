Your Child's Care Journey

A complete, parent-friendly guide from initial diagnostic screening to complete recovery.

Care Process

Step-by-Step Congenital Care

We walk hand-in-hand with parents to ensure absolute clarity, safety, and a calm healing environment for every child.

1. Initial Evaluation & Echocardiography

The journey begins with non-invasive screenings. Dr. Shukla performs pediatric Doppler echo tests or fetal echo screens to evaluate cardiac structures, trace flow velocities, and pinpoint congenital defects precisely without any pain or radiation.

2. Parent Counseling & Detailed Discussion

Once the diagnosis is established, Dr. Shukla hosts a detailed counseling session. Using anatomical diagrams, he explains the condition to parents, addresses doubts, and discusses treatment routes (monitoring vs. non-surgical device closure).

3. Pre-Procedure Workup

If a catheter closure is planned, parents receive a structured list of checks: blood scans, clinical pediatric parameters verification, and fasting guidelines (nil by mouth for 4-6 hours before the intervention).

4. Min-Invasive Catheter Closure

Inside the specialized cardiac cath lab, the child receives mild sedation. Dr. Shukla carefully inserts a tiny catheter through a leg vein, deploying an occlusion device (PDA, ASD, or VSD plug) to seal the defect without surgery or stitches.

5. Intensive Monitoring & Discharge

The child recovers in the pediatric cardiac ICU under Dr. Shukla's close care. Most children are active within hours. A follow-up echo scan is performed to confirm device stability before discharging the child within 24 hours.

6. Follow-up & Healthy Active Life

Structured checkups are scheduled at 1 month, 6 months, and 1 year. Once recovery is complete, the child can return to normal play and activities with no long-term physical restrictions.
