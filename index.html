Care Process

Congenital Care Journey

A clear, supportive step-by-step pathway designed to comfort and guide families through diagnosis and recovery.

01
Non-Invasive Diagnostic Screen

Advanced diagnostic evaluation using fetal echocardiography or pediatric Doppler scans to map heart structures precisely.

02
Empathetic Family Counseling

Detailed sessions explaining the diagnosis, resolving parental queries, and charting out the best tailored treatment options.

03
Minimally Invasive Procedure

Catheter-guided structural occlusion (such as PDA or ASD closure device deployment) or valvuloplasty inside specialized cardiac labs.

04
Specialized Post-Op ICU Care

Close, continuous monitoring by Dr. Shukla as a specialized pediatric intensivist to ensure absolute safety and stability.

05
Safe Recovery & Active Follow-up

Most patients are discharged within 24 hours of catheter closures, returning to active, healthy lives with structured checkups.
